1Claim주장 · tells our opinion
How to write a claim

Step 1Give your opinion.

In my opinion,
I believe [think] that
I do not believe [think] that

Step 2Say which one is better or worse.

A is better / worse (for ______) than B

TipWant a stronger claim?

should + base verb
→ 꼭 해야 할 일을 말할 때 (강한 주장)
e.g. Schools should keep school uniforms.
Model Text

School Uniforms

In my opinion, school uniforms are better for students than everyday clothes. One reason is that school uniforms can save time. For example, students do not need to choose clothes every morning. Another reason is that school uniforms can make students feel like a team. For example, all students wear the same clothes at school events. In conclusion, school uniforms are a smart choice for students.

Step을 하나씩 열며 설명하고, "What's step 1? step 2? step 3?"로 확인해요. 과제는 말로 지시해요. 오른쪽 모델 글은 보여주기만 하는 시각 자료예요.
2Reason & Evidence이유 tells WHY · 근거 gives an example
How to write a reason

Step 1Start the reason.

One reason is that
Another reason is that

Step 2Say what it can do.

can + base verb
→ 할 수 있는 일 · 가능한 결과를 말할 때
e.g. School uniforms can save time.
How to write evidence

Step 3Start the evidence.

For example,
For instance,

Step 4Give a real example.

Who does what? (실제 장면)
e.g. Students do not need to choose clothes every morning.
→ 이 장면이 이유가 맞다는 걸 보여줘요.

3Conclusion결론 · says our claim again in different words
How to write a conclusion

Step 1Start the conclusion.

In conclusion,
To sum up,
For these reasons,

Step 2Keep the same opinion.

Do not change your position.

Step 3Change the key words.

e.g. better → a smart choice
Model Text

School Uniforms

In my opinion, school uniforms are better for students than everyday clothes. One reason is that school uniforms can save time. For example, students do not need to choose clothes every morning. Another reason is that school uniforms can make students feel like a team. For example, all students wear the same clothes at school events. In conclusion, school uniforms are a smart choice for students.

Step 3에서 Paraphrase 탭의 Conclusion을 띄우면 Claim → Conclusion으로 바뀐 부분을 크게 보여줄 수 있어요.
